How the calculation works

Every figure below comes from the workbook's allocation model. Expand it to see the logic and adjust any of the assumptions.

For a given role and WTE, the model looks up two things: available hours — the working capacity that WTE represents each month — and a set of planned activity hours — antenatal visits, reviews, clinics, supervision, meetings and so on, each with its own monthly hours. Adding up the activity hours gives planned hours; available − planned is the spare or over capacity you see in the team table. The three roles (Health Visitor, Band 5, Band 4) each have their own set of activities and hours.

The numbers below are the full-time (1.0 WTE) figures — everything scales down proportionally for part-time staff. Change any figure to reflect local time-allocation data; the team table, map and charts update immediately. Use Reset to workbook defaults to undo your changes for a role.
